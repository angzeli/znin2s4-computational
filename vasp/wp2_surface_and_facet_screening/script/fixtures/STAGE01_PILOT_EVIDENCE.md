# Stage 01 frozen regression evidence

`stage01_pilot_evidence.npz` contains numerical arrays and selected metadata from
the five archived A0/A1/B0/A2/B1 calculations. Load with `allow_pickle=False`.
`stage01_pilot_expectations.csv` preserves the independent expected values and
native-file hashes recorded on 15 September 2026. Source paths are relative to
the WP2 directory. These fixtures contain no PAW datasets or executables.

The missing fixture was reconstructed on 22 September 2026 from the original
campaign records under
`/Volumes/Scratch/wp2_surface_facet_screening_runs/01_runtime_pilot/analysis/`:

- `20260915-diagnostic-correction-9xgql54r/pilot_native_arrays.npz`: unchanged
  EIGENVAL/XML energies, occupations, k points, weights, forces, SCF counts and
  native metadata. The expectations CSV was recovered from `final-task.diff`
  in the same directory, preserving its recorded values and hashes.
- `20260915-results-consolidation-qidqukfw/before/scientific_audit/`: the five
  planar-profile CSVs and independent `vacuum_window_checks.csv`. Profile arrays
  retain the native samples from 1 Å inside each cell boundary to 2 Å outside
  each outermost atom; the 4/5/6 Å windows are subsets. Potential and density
  units are eV and electrons/Å³. No interpolation is applied.
- Final CONTCAR files at the CSV's source paths supply slab extrema and cell
  height. All five SHA-256 identities were verified against the frozen CSV.

For each pilot prefix, `expected_window_metrics`, `expected_window_status` and
`expected_automatic_status` preserve the independent window CSV in setback
2/4/5/6 Å and lower/upper order. `window_metric_columns` records numeric column
order. Expected values were not generated by the functions under test.

This is a reconstruction from authentic archived components, not a claim of
byte identity to the missing original NPZ. The frozen expectations retain the
original frontier, force and window tolerances. The separate
`stage01_electrostatics.json` was restored unchanged from the earlier audit's
`before/script/fixtures/` directory.

The optional protected-native-file inventory test requires
`WP2_INTEGRITY_MANIFEST`; ordinary fixture tests do not require that inventory.
